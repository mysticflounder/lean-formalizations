## Verification
- Authors: ✅ "P. Erdős, Stanford University" (title page).
- Title: ✅ "ON SETS OF DISTANCES OF n POINTS."
- Venue: ⚠️ The scanned article does not show the journal masthead on p.1; the
  text (Theorem 1, f(n) bounds) matches the known Amer. Math. Monthly 53 (1946)
  paper. Page range not independently confirmable from the scan.
- Content claims: ✅ "Origin of the distinct-distances problem." Confirmed: the
  paper defines f(n) = min number of distinct distances among n planar points and
  proves the first bounds `(n−3/4)^{1/2} − 1/2 < f(n) < c·n/(log n)^{1/2}`
  (Theorem 1) — this is the founding statement of the Erdős distinct-distances
  problem, the target of the Elekes–Sharir reduction formalized in
  `ElekesSharirGuthKatz/`.
